The user's position (2026-10-26): covering the cube is the goal. Mathematically it demonstrates
that the methods (routes, determinate weak maps, per-arrow strong forms, canonical models that name
objects where they first appear) handle many cases; philosophically the cube covers most of what
people care about.

**Why:** asked whether the cube is mathematically special; the answer was "mostly tradition" (only
S4/S5 are deep on their own; Sahlqvist uniformity covers far more; the cube is roughly four Horn
closure conditions plus seriality), and the user framed the project's scope this way in reply.

**How to apply:** plan and prioritise toward completing the cube (5, B + 4, S5).  Don't steer the
work toward a general theorem for all Horn closure conditions, or toward `.2`, density etc.; mention
such extensions only as asides or future work.  Related: [[feedback_qml_principled_over_minimal]]
(principled designs are still preferred *within* the cube), [[project_qml_current_position]].
